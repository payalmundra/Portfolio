### 1. Netflix Content Strategy & Recommendation Engine
**Strategic Analysis | NLP | Machine Learning**
* **Situation:** Netflix needed to leverage its 8,800+ title catalog to uncover under-served global markets and improve user retention in a saturated streaming landscape.
* **Task:** To build a Content-Based Recommendation System and perform a strategic gap analysis for content acquisition.
* **Action:** Engineered a recommendation model using TF-IDF & Cosine Similarity to match movies based on plot semantics and conducted a "Supply vs. Demand" analysis of regional libraries.

### 2. Aerofit Customer Segmentation & Sales Strategy
**Probability Analysis | Statistical Testing | Random Forest**
* **Situation:** Aerofit, a fitness equipment brand, struggled with inefficient ad spend due to undefined customer personas across its Entry-Level, Mid-Tier, and Premium treadmill lines.
* **Task:** To build quantitative Customer Profiles using probability theory and develop a Predictive Model to target high-value users.
* **Action:**
   * Profiling (Probability & Statistics): Calculated Marginal, Joint & Conditional Probabilities to identify key buyer traits and validated segment overlaps using Kruskal-Wallis Hypothesis Testing.
   * Modeling (ML): Reframed the problem to binary classification (Mass Market vs. Premium) and trained a Random Forest Classifier to predict purchase probability.
* **Result:**
   * Commercial Insight: Established that high-income, high-fitness users are the exclusive target for Premium products, enabling a targeted upselling strategy.
   * Model Performance: Achieved a 94% accuracy rate (up from a 64% baseline) by simplifying target variables based on the probability analysis.
* 🔗 **[View Project Repository](https://github.com/payalmundra/Aerofit-Customer-Segmentation)**

### 3. E-Commerce Retail Analysis: Insights & Strategic Growth
**SQL | Tableau | Business Strategy**
* **Situation:** Analyzed a dataset of 100,000+ orders for a retailer experiencing a 137% revenue surge, where undefined customer personas and logistics inefficiencies threatened sustainable growth.
* **Task:** To perform a comprehensive audit of the business—optimizing supply chain logistics, marketing seasonality, and payment strategies.
* **Action:** Logistics Engineering: Used SQL Window Functions (DENSE_RANK) to audit delivery routes, uncovering a 15-day over-estimation gap in northern states.
* **Customer Profiling:** Analyzed payment behavior to find a sharp drop-off after 10 installments and identified "Afternoons" and months like November/January as peak demand windows.
* **Result:** Validated the business case for a "Hub-and-Spoke" fulfillment model to fix delivery delays and proposed a targeted 3-10 month EMI strategy to capture the 49,000+ mass-market users.
* 🔗 **[View Project Repository](https://github.com/payalmundra/data-analysis-of-leading-supermarket-in-brazil)**
